### Dr. Stacy Sims — Nutrition

**On body comp at 150 lbs, 12 days from race:**

First, context: you just completed peak week — the highest training load of the entire block. Your body has been actively storing glycogen and retaining water in proportion to that load. This is not fat gain. This is your body doing exactly what it needs to do to perform on July 11.

Taper physiology makes this more pronounced, not less. As volume drops this week, glycogen stores fill even further (especially as carbs stay high or increase). The scale will likely not move down — and should not. A lighter scale reading right now would actually be a warning sign.

Postpartum recomp operates on a different timeline than standard recomp. With milk production ongoing, your body holds onto fat reserves as a protective mechanism — this is evolutionary, not a failure of effort. Meaningful body comp change in this context happens over months, not weeks, and is most visible after weaning or hormonal stabilization.

**Taper week nutrition (now through July 5):**
- Calories: hold at 2,100–2,300 kcal. Do NOT reduce because volume is lower.
- Protein: 150g every day — tissue repair happens during taper, not training.
- Carbs: move toward 195g starting July 6 for glycogen loading.
- If you feel the urge to eat less because you're doing less — don't. That's the most common taper mistake.

**Supply watch:** if anything dips in the next 5 days, carbs go up first, not last.
