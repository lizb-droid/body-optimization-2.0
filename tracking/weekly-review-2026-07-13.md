**Supplement upgrades — both approved:**
- Momentous Omega-3 (800mg DHA / 800mg EPA): Strong call. The Sports Research dose (130mg DHA) was inadequate for breastfeeding — DHA is preferentially channeled to breast milk and infant neurodevelopment depends on it. The new dose clears the threshold. Take with a fat-containing meal.
- NOW Foods NAC 600mg: Mechanism is solid (glutathione precursor, oxidative stress reduction post-hard-effort). **Phase 4 altitude adjustments — start now:**
- Carbs: increase by 20-25g on training days. Fat oxidation is oxygen-dependent and blunted at altitude. Carbohydrate becomes the dominant fuel. Training-day target moves to 195-200g carbs.
- Hydration: add 16-24oz above current daily floor. Altitude increases respiratory water loss. Pre-load sodium before every hard session.
- Recovery window: 25g protein + 25g carbs within 30 minutes post-session. At altitude, the anabolic window closes faster under hypoxic stress. Have it packed before the session starts.
- Milk supply: some nursing mothers see supply fluctuation in the first 3-5 days at altitude. Add 20-30g carbs immediately if any drop is noticed.

**Action item — before the Phase 4 block starts:** Get ferritin tested this week. Altitude training drives up red blood cell synthesis, which is iron-dependent. If ferritin is under 30 ng/mL, altitude adaptation will be blunted and fatigue will hit by Week 2. This is the most common and most preventable altitude block failure point.
