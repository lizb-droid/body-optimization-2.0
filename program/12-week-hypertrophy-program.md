# 12-Week Hypertrophy Program
**Mon Nov 9, 2026 – Sun Jan 31, 2027 · Muscle-Building Phase**

Goal: build as much muscle as possible this block, with a visibly leaner, ripped physique as the one-year outcome.
Priority order: Glutes → Shoulders → Legs → Core
60-minute sessions, warm-up included (hard stop) · 5 lifting days + 1 trail run + 1 rest

---

## How You Get Stronger: The Progression System

Two different rules apply depending on the equipment, because dumbbells jump in bigger increments than barbells do.

### Barbell main lifts — straight weight progression
(Back Squat, Hip Thrust, Bench Press, Bent Over Row, Push Press/Jerk, Thrusters, Cyclist Squat)

Add small plates (2.5-5 lb per side) whenever you hit **all sets at the top of the rep range with 2+ reps still left in the tank** (RPE ≤ 8). If a set was genuinely hard (RPE 9+), repeat that weight next week before adding. If you miss reps two sessions in a row, drop 10% and rebuild from there.

### Dumbbell / accessory work — rep target changes by week
(Curls, DB Shoulder Press, Rear Delt Fly, Tricep work, Squeeze Press, Floor Press, Split Squats, Lunges, etc.)

The target rep count itself shifts week to week within each 5-week loading block. Early weeks you work at a lower rep count on a heavier dumbbell. Later weeks the target climbs: same weight, more reps, more time under tension. When the block resets after the deload, jump to the next dumbbell and start the low-rep week again.

**Weekly rep target — standard wave:**

| Week in block | Target reps | What you're doing |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 8 | New weight (or first week back post-deload): real work by rep 8 |
| Week 2 | 9 | Same weight, one more rep |
| Week 3 | 10 | Same weight, building |
| Week 4 | 11 | Same weight, close to the ceiling |
| Week 5 | 12 | Top of the block. If every set hits 12 cleanly, jump weight next block |

Two loading blocks: Weeks 1-5 and Weeks 7-11. The table runs twice, resetting at Week 7.

**Worked example — Incline DB Curl:**
- Week 1: 25 lb × 8 · Week 2: ×9 · Week 3: ×10 · Week 4: ×11 · Week 5: ×12 (topped out)
- Week 6: deload, hold 25 lb, don't chase reps
- Week 7: 30 lb × 8, new block, reps reset

If the next dumbbell is too big a jump (you hit 12 and the heavier weight only gets 4-5 reps), stay at the current weight one more block and push reps past 12, or use a smaller increment if your dumbbells allow it.

**Higher-rep isolation wave** (DB Lateral Raise, Seated Rear Delt Fly, Spider Curl, GHD Hip Extension, Slider Hamstring Curl):

| Week in block | Target reps |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 10 |
| Week 2 | 11 |
| Week 3 | 12 |
| Week 4 | 13 |
| Week 5 | 14, top out, jump weight next block |

**Effort rule for hypertrophy:** on the last set of every accessory, finish 1-2 reps shy of failure. The wave sets the reps; this sets how hard they are.

## Weekly Structure

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Lower A — Glute Focus |
| Tuesday | Upper A — Chest + Back |
| Wednesday | Lower B — Quads + Hamstrings |
| Thursday | Upper B — Shoulders + Chest |
| Friday | Trail Run — Zone 2 |
| Saturday | Full Body — Pull + Glutes |
| Sunday | Rest |

**Deload weeks:** Week 6 and Week 12. Volume -40%, intensity -15%, stop 2-4 reps shy of failure on everything. You are not detraining. You're letting accumulated fatigue clear so the next block hits harder. Hold the same weights from the week before. Don't add, don't test failure, and don't skip it because you feel fine.

**Holidays:** train as normal. Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26) is Upper B in Week 3. Christmas (Fri Dec 25) and New Year's Day (Fri Jan 1) are trail-run days in Weeks 7 and 8.

## MONDAY — Lower A (Glute Focus)

**Warm-up** (~4 min)
- World's Greatest Stretch — 30s each side
- Glute Bridge March — 30s
- Hip Circles — 30s each side
- Posterior Hip Capsule Stretch — 45s each side
- Squat to Stand — 30s

**Main Lift** — Barbell Back Squat 4×6 · 2:30-3 min rest
*Straight weight progression. Your heaviest lower-body lift of the week. Full rest matters.*

**Main Lift 2** — Barbell Hip Thrust 4×10 (2s hold) · 2 min rest
*Your #1 priority movement. The 2-second hold keeps tension on the glute at peak contraction.*

**Superset A** — 3 rounds, 60s rest
Single-leg DB RDL ea *(standard wave, 8→12)* / Deficit Reverse Lunge ea, dumbbells, off a plate *(standard wave, 8→12)*

**Superset B** — 3 rounds, 60s rest
Lateral Lunge ea *(standard wave, 8→12)* / Weighted Dead Bug ×8 ea, light DB in the extended arm

**Finisher** — Every 2 min × 4 rounds
10 KB Swings + 8 Walking Lunges ea

*Deload week: 3×6 squat, 3×10 hip thrust, 2 rounds each superset, skip finisher. Hold last week's weights.*

**Superset B** — 3 rounds, 60s rest
Arnold Press *(standard wave, 8→12)* / DB Rear Delt Fly *(standard wave, 8→12)*

DB Lateral Raise *(higher-rep wave, 10→14)* / Incline DB Curl *(standard wave, 8→12)* / Weighted Copenhagen Plank ×20s ea *(DR-safe core rotation)*

*No finisher. Tuesday runs to the full 60 minutes without one.*

*Deload week: 3×8 each main lift, 2 rounds each superset. Hold last week's weights.*

## FRIDAY — Trail Run (Zone 2)

Conversational pace, up to 4 miles. Walk the uphills, run the flats and downhills. Same every week.

- If your quads are sore from Wednesday, walk the downhills. Saturday is full body.
- Sharp or joint pain: stop and walk it in.
- **Deload weeks (6, 12):** 2-3 miles or all walking.

## Tracking

- Weigh yourself 3-4 mornings a week and use the average.
- Waist and hip measurements plus photos every two weeks.
- Log every main lift (weight, reps, RPE).
- **Week 4 check-in:** review weight trend and lifts with the nutritionist and coach. If weight is flat or dropping and lifts stall, add 100-150 kcal, mostly carbs. If you're gaining more than about a pound a week, we trim.

---

## Guardrails and Monitoring

- **Pelvic floor:** leaking, pressure, or heaviness during lifting or running means stop that movement and regress it now.
- **Milk supply:** a 3-day dip means add 20-30g carbs first, then a calorie and load audit before anything else.
- **Weight drop:** more than 3 lb in a week is a flag. Fueling audit before load audit.
- **Joint laxity:** film your top Back Squat set every other week and check knee tracking under fatigue.
- **Autoregulated deload:** if Squat Set 1 feels RPE 8+ when the target is RPE 6-7, make it a deload day.
- **Copenhagen Plank:** progress slowly and watch for pubic symphysis discomfort.
- **Overhead pressing:** if the shoulder feels loose, reduce load and do extra band pull-aparts first.
- **Lower back:** Barbell RDL stays out of the program. Single-leg DB RDL is the hinge pattern.
- **Pain:** sharp or joint pain means stop, swap, and reassess.
