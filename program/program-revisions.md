## Wednesday Race Recovery Protocol

Wednesday is the highest weekly stress day. Formalize recovery:

- **Within 30 min post-race:** 25g protein + 30g carbs (mandatory — milk supply protection)
- **Thursday:** No running, no gym. Walk only.
- **Friday Upper B gate:** If Thursday soreness is >5/10 → drop 1 set from all main lifts + skip accessories. Upper B is sacrificial — protect Monday and Tuesday above all.

---

## Key Reasoning

**Lower A time fix:** Original ran 66–73 min. Cuts always fall on accessory work you actually need. Revised targets 54–58 min with warm-up buffer.

**GHD before Hip Thrust:** GHD primes the posterior chain neurally. Doing it after Hip Thrust 4×12 means erectors are already fatigued. Reversed = better primer, lower injury risk as loads increase.

**DB Pullover → Chest-Supported Row:** Original push:pull ratio ~3:1. Breastfeeding posture = chronic anterior shoulder load. Suppressed estrogen affects rotator cuff tendon health. Chest-supported row targets mid-traps and rhomboids — exactly the postural muscles weakened by nursing.

**Power movements first:** Peak power output window at 5 AM is ~20 minutes. Power quality degrades faster than strength quality — protect the window.

**No Phase 4 power add-ons:** Only 5 weeks between Cirque Snowbird (Jul 11) and Cirque Alta (Aug 15). Insufficient time to build and supercompensate. Attempting this risks carrying fatigue into Wk 17 taper. Maintain and sharpen what was built in Phase 2–3.

**Power add-on cutoff = Wk 10:** Last week of power add-ons is Wk 10 (peak week, Solitude Jun 24). Wk 11 is deload — no power work. Wks 12 + 17 are race weeks — no power work. Effectively: hit power hard this week (Wk 9) and Wk 10, then stop.

**Phase 4 Upper B volume reduction:** Upper B is already the sacrificial session. During Wks 14–16 maintenance, drop main lifts from 4 → 3 working sets. Accessories stay at 3×12. Goal is maintenance, not accumulation — protect recovery for Alta prep.

**Race-day calorie bump:** Lower A Mondays and all race days target 2,400–2,500 kcal (vs. standard 2,100–2,300). Training load + race effort = higher burn. Milk supply is the leading indicator — if it dips after a race week, calorie audit is step one.

**Intra-workout carbs (Phase 2+):** As training intensity increases, pre-workout snack may not sustain session quality AND protect milk supply. 20–30g fast carbs in last 15 min blunts the cortisol spike when glycogen drops. Directly protects prolactin levels.

### Why

**Step-up with High Knee Drive (#27):** Direct carryover to uphill trail mechanics. The knee drive replicates the stride cycle on steep grades. Replacing Walking Lunges removes a movement that offers less specificity while adding eccentric quad fatigue that doesn't transfer. Already confirmed in today's session.

**Bird Dog (#28):** Deep stabilization for uneven terrain — activates TA and multifidus without spinal flexion. Safe at 1-finger DR gap. Supsets with Pallof Hold.

**Pallof Hold (#29):** Anti-rotation stability transfers directly to technical trail running and pole use. Resistance band replaces cable; no new equipment needed.

**Daily rehab (#30):** 1-finger DR gap is healing but not closed. Daily sub-threshold activation accelerates closure without adding training load. Pelvic floor primer for the rest of the day.

**Alta Saturdays (#31):** 6 Saturday runs between now and race day is the primary altitude adaptation block. Snowbird DNF was a specific physiological ceiling — altitude + sustained steep grade — not a fitness failure. Alta trails (8,000–11,000 ft) address the exact stimulus. 60–70 min keeps volume in range; steepness is the variable.

**Wednesday climbing intervals (#32):** This is the highest-leverage change. Race analysis shows progressive lap splits (23:06 → 27:49 → 37:15) driven by steepening gradient, not glycogen or cardiac drift. The fix is sustained steep climbing under fatigue. 4–6 × 3–4 min hard uphill with walk-down recovery trains the aerobic system to hold power on grade.

**Leki poles (#33):** Pole technique degrades without repetition. Six weeks of practice is minimum — don't wait. Replace before next Wednesday.

### What to Watch For

- Bird Dog: watch for breath-holding or rib flare — either means load is too high, reduce range of motion
- Pallof Hold: if any pelvic floor pressure → stop, regress to Dead Bug only
- Wednesday intervals: HR should be 85–92% max (zone 4); if unable to complete 3 min at effort after 4 rounds, cut the session — don't push through dead legs the day before Thursday rest
- Saturday Alta: if hip flexor or anterior knee pain after first 2 sessions, reduce grade or duration before cutting volume
- Weight drop: if body weight falls >3 lbs in a week during the training intensification → flag immediately (fueling audit before load audit)
