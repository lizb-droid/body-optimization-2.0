# Coach script

Everything the coach says during a guided session. Rewrite any line in your own words and leave the ones you like. Once it is final, it gets wired into the app.

Words in `{curly brackets}` get filled in automatically. Keep them where you want the number or name to be spoken. Lines are short on purpose: one idea each, easy to hear mid-set.

| Placeholder | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `{session}` | Workout name, e.g. "Push. Shoulders and chest" |
| `{minutes}` | Total minutes, e.g. "48" |
| `{section}` | Section name, e.g. "Superset A" |
| `{exercise}` | Exercise name |
| `{reps}` | Reps or time, e.g. "8 to 12 reps", "20 second hold" |
| `{set}` / `{sets}` | Current set number / total sets |
| `{round}` / `{rounds}` | Current round / total rounds |
| `{rest}` | Rest length, e.g. "2 minutes" |
| `{seconds}` | Warm-up move length |
| `{side}` | "left" or "right" |
| `{cue}` | That exercise's coaching cue (from the list further down) |

## Exercise cues (`{cue}`)

Read during the section preview and when you set up for a lift.

- **Arnold Press:** Full rotation — start palms facing you, end palms forward at the top.
- **Band Pull-Apart:** Arms straight. Squeeze the shoulder blades. No shrugging.
- **Barbell Back Squat:** Heaviest lower-body lift. Full rest matters — don't sacrifice load for a shorter session.
- **Barbell Bench Press:** Straight weight progression.
- **Barbell Bent Over Row:** Chest up, hinge at the hip — primary back-thickness builder.
- **Barbell Cyclist Squat:** Heels elevated — shifts emphasis to quads. Different angle than Monday's Back Squat.
- **Barbell Hip Thrust:** 2s hold at the top. Your #1 priority movement — keep tension on the glute at peak contraction.
- **Bird Dog:** Anti-extension and anti-rotation from all-fours. DR-safe core rotation — slow and controlled.
- **Bulgarian Split Squat:** Drop straight down. Front heel stays planted. Drive through it.
- **Chest-Supported DB Row:** Chest braced on incline bench — zero lower-back involvement, pure lat isolation.
- **DB Floor Press:** Chest-dominant, different primary muscle than DB Shoulder Press. No bench needed — silent.
- **DB High Pull:** Vertical pulling plane — hits traps and rear delt differently than any row in the program. Controlled tempo; no need to be explosive.
- **DB Lateral Raise:** Lead with the elbows. Stop at shoulder height. No swinging.
- **DB Pullover:** Lying flat, arms tracking closer together — arc from above the chest to behind the head. Targets lats and rib cage stretch. Different plane of motion than any row. Start at 45lb; extend reps to ceiling before jumping to 55lb.
- **DB Rear Delt Fly:** Hinge forward, soft elbows. Squeeze the back of the shoulder.
- **DB Row tripod:** Brace on the bench. Pull the elbow to the hip.
- **DB Shoulder Press:** Ribs down, glutes tight. Press straight up.
- **DB Snatch:** Hips drive it. Punch overhead and lock out.
- **DB Squeeze Press:** Both DBs pressed together throughout — constant inward tension, different feel than any other press.
- **DB Thrusters:** Squat deep. Drive up and press in one motion.
- **DB Z Press:** Seated on the floor, legs straight, no leg drive — strict core-braced overhead press.
- **Deficit Reverse Lunge:** Step back with control. Front heel stays down.
- **Dips:** Bodyweight to start. Add a weighted vest or hold a DB once bodyweight reps feel easy.
- **Farmer Carry:** Tall posture. Ribs down. Short, quick steps.
- **GHD Hip Extension:** Squeeze the glutes at the top. Don't over-arch the lower back.
- **Incline DB Curl:** Elbows back. Slow lowering. Feel the stretch.
- **KB Swing:** Hips, not arms. Snap the hips and let the bell float.
- **Lateral Lunge:** Sit back into the hip. Toes forward, chest up.
- **Overhead Tricep Extension:** Elbows close. Full stretch, full lockout.
- **Pallof Hold:** Anti-rotation — hold steady. Band tension, not load. This is a core control exercise.
- **Push Press + Push Jerk:** Load at a weight you can strict press 4–5 reps. The Push Jerk reps should feel comparatively easier.
- **Push-up:** Body in one line. Chest to the floor.
- **Row:** Legs first, then back, then arms.
- **Seated Rear Delt Fly:** Lead with the elbows. Squeeze. No momentum.
- **Single-leg DB RDL:** Same hip-hinge as Landmine RDL — simpler setup, no barbell. Balance challenge adds proprioception.
- **Slider Hamstring Curl:** Hips high. Slow on the way out. Pull the heels in with the hamstrings.
- **Spider Curl:** Chest on the bench. Squeeze at the top.
- **Suitcase Carry:** Don't lean. Stay tall, ribs stacked.
- **Thrusters:** Build into it with lighter warm-up sets before your working 5×5.
- **Turkish Get-Up:** Eyes on the hand. Slow. One piece at a time.
- **Walking Lunge:** Long step. Back knee kisses the floor.
- **Weighted Copenhagen Plank:** Anti-lateral-flexion core. Hold steady, resist hip drop. DR-safe.
- **Weighted Dead Bug:** Light DB in the extended arm — same DR-safe pattern as bodyweight Dead Bug, higher core demand.

## Warm-up moves (optional, adds a line after the name)

- **World's Greatest Stretch:** Reach up and rotate. Open the hips.
- **Glute Bridge March:** Hips stay level. Squeeze each rep.
- **Hip Circles:** Big, slow circles.
- **Posterior Hip Capsule Stretch:** Sink back. Breathe into the hip.
- **Squat to Stand:** Fold down, chest up, stand tall.
- **Cat Cow:** Move with your breath.
- **Child's Pose Thread the Needle:** Reach through. Open the upper back.
- **Scapular Push-up:** Arms straight. Shoulder blades do the work.
- **Push-up to Down Dog:** Flow it. Press the floor away.
- **Band Pull-Apart:** Wake up the back of the shoulders.
